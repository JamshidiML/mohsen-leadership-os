# AGENTS.md

## Mission
Maintain Mohsen Leadership OS as a practical, privacy-preserving, evidence-based three-year leadership development system.

## Governance
- Mohsen is the final decision-maker.
- ChatGPT owns strategy, behavioral design and governance review.
- Codex owns implementation, tests and repository maintenance.
- Important changes use a dedicated branch and Draft PR.
- Never merge, close a gate, or claim success without Mohsen's explicit approval.

## Safety rules
- Do not diagnose or treat mental-health conditions.
- Never score, profile or optimize the spouse or child. Track only Mohsen's commitments and observable behavior.
- Never store private messages, names, medical details, company secrets or conflict transcripts in Git history.
- Never design manipulative, coercive, deceptive or surveillance-based tactics.
- Prefer small executable improvements over architecture-heavy expansion.

## Delivery standards
- Keep independent weekly workload at 3–4 hours maximum.
- Generated weekly plans must contain no more than three major outcomes.
- Every behavioral task needs observable evidence and a review question.
- Every important PR states: user outcome, behavioral mechanism, evidence required, privacy impact, validation and rollback plan.
